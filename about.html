About

Evenfaring is a one-person studio building small, useful apps with care.

How the studio works

  • Small and focused

    Each app does one thing and tries to do it well.

  • Built slowly

    Apps ship when they are ready, then get steady, careful updates.

  • Honest pricing

    No ads. Core features stay free. Any paid extras are optional and clearly priced up front.

  • Privacy by design

    Apps collect only what they need. Each one has a plain-language privacy policy, and your data is never sold.
