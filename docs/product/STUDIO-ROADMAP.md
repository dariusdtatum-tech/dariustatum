## Principles (apply to every app from the studio)

- No ads. Ever. In a recovery context, ad networks cannot guarantee what a vulnerable user will be shown; one wrong ad ends trust permanently.
- Never gate the core help. For Urge Walk, the urge button and the walk are always free. Monetization may only ever attach to extras around the core (deeper history, exports, themes, structured programs) or to supporter-style purchases.
- Collect nothing by default. Apps are local-first: user data stays on the device. Improvement signal comes from conversation, not surveillance — the Urge Walk Discord server, TestFlight feedback, App Store reviews, and App Store Connect's aggregated metrics. Any future diagnostic must be opt-in, content-free, and disclosed in the privacy policy before it ships.
- Not therapy, not treatment. The studio's apps are self-guided support tools. Copy claims support, never outcomes; crisis and professional-help resources stay visible.
- No shaming, ever. No streak guilt, no alarm design, no urgency tricks (per the locked design rules).
- Each app markets the others. The portfolio is the strategy: modest, honest revenue from several calm instruments funds the next one.

## The improvement loop

- TestFlight for the fast loop while developing; App Review for every public update; phased release for anything risky.
